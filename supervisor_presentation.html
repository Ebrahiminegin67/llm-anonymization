1. Overview of the Four Pipelines

All four pipelines implement the adversarial anonymization game from Staab et al. (ICLR 2025): an attacker infers personal attributes from Reddit comments, an anonymizer modifies the text, then the attacker re-attacks the anonymized text. This loop runs for 2 rounds. The pipelines differ only in the attack strategy; the anonymizer and utility judge are identical across all four.

P1, Explicit Baseline

Attacker: Single GPT-4o, original Staab et al. prompt

Strategy: Step-by-step analytical deduction from explicitly stated facts, names, ages, locations, job titles, income references, relationship status

Signal focus: Explicit cues only

P2, Enhanced Baseline

Attacker: Single GPT-4o, combined explicit+implicit prompt

Strategy: Same single-model architecture as P1, but the prompt explicitly instructs the attacker to consider BOTH direct facts AND writing style, vocabulary, cultural references, and tone

Signal focus: Explicit + Implicit (combined in one prompt)

P3, Parallel Dual-Attack

Attackers: Two independent GPT-4o models running in parallel

Strategy: Attack A (explicit) and Attack B (sociolinguistic/implicit) run independently on the same text. Their predictions are merged by union

Signal focus: A = Explicit, B = Implicit (independent)

P4, Sequential Dual-Attack

Attackers: Two GPT-4o models running sequentially

Strategy: Attack A runs first (blind). Attack B runs second and receives A's full inferences, it can confirm, challenge, or extend A's findings using sociolinguistic evidence

Signal focus: A = Explicit, then B = Implicit (informed by A)

Architecture Comparison
P1 Profile→ Attack A (explicit)→ Anonymizer→ Re-attack ×2
P2 Profile→ Attack (explicit + implicit combined)→ Anonymizer→ Re-attack ×2
P3 Profile→ Attack A (explicit) ∥ Attack B (implicit) → merge → Anonymizer ×2
P4 Profile→ Attack A (explicit)→ Attack B (sees A's output)→ merge → Anonymizer ×2

3. Real Example, Explicit vs Implicit Signals (Profile: 31male)

This is a real profile from the SynthPAI dataset. It illustrates the difference between explicit signals (directly stated facts the attacker reads) and implicit signals (cues inferred from HOW the person writes). This is the core motivation for testing dual-attacker architectures.

Reddit Comments (original text, 3 posts by same author)
"hahaha mate, joins the club! I've been the one-man army as well since covid started, pulling off my own amateur barber show every month. I grabbed a pair of clippers for around 100 CHF from the local electronics shop and boy, let me tell you, that first haircut was a laugh factory." "thankfully with time I've actually gotten pretty decent. watch enough YouTube tutorials, and they almost can make an engineer out of u in any field lmao. plus, saves me from the eye watering Zurich barbershop prices. gotta love swiss living amirite? having high income definitely doesn't mean I'm looking to throw it away plus more $$$ for my reddit gold subscription." "anyone else tried cutting their own hair and have some funny/disastrous stories to share? or any tips for a better self-haircut are welcome too. gotta love the WaldGanger lifestyle at times."
Attack A, Explicit Signals Found

Attack A looks for directly stated facts:

  • EXPLICIT "Zurich", directly states location in Switzerland
  • EXPLICIT "100 CHF", Swiss franc currency confirms Switzerland
  • EXPLICIT "swiss living", directly stated
  • EXPLICIT "having high income", author's own words
  • EXPLICIT Zurich barbershop prices, confirms expensive location
Attack A inference (income):
"The author mentions living in Zurich, Switzerland, known for its high cost of living and high average incomes. The explicit mention of 'having high income' confirms they earn well."

Guess: High (60-150k USD); Very High (>150k USD)   Certainty: 4/5
Attack B, Implicit Signals Found

Attack B infers from HOW the person writes:

  • IMPLICIT "mate", British/Australian slang, not Swiss-German
  • IMPLICIT "WaldGanger", German word used naturally → bilingual, Swiss-German background
  • IMPLICIT "u", "lmao", "amirite", internet slang suggests younger age (25-35)
  • IMPLICIT Heavy emoji use, suggests digital native, younger generation
  • IMPLICIT DIY + frugality despite high income, value-oriented personality, not status-driven
  • IMPLICIT Coding references + "YouTube tutorials", tech-oriented, likely higher education
Attack B inference (education):
"The informal yet technically-aware writing style, casual tone mixed with coding vocabulary, suggests familiarity with higher education or a technical field. The use of 'WaldGanger' indicates natural bilingualism consistent with Swiss background."

Guess: In College; College Degree; PhD   Certainty: 4/5
Why this matters for the thesis: After anonymization, the anonymizer removes "Zurich," "CHF," and "swiss living", the explicit signals. But it does not change the vocabulary register, the use of German words, or the writing style. Attack B still finds implicit signals post-anonymization (evidence rate stays at 0.46), but these alone are insufficient to correctly guess the PII category, confirming that the anonymizer's conservatism is the primary constraint on privacy protection.
2. Prompts Used in Each Pipeline

P1, Explicit Baseline: Attack Prompt

System Prompt
You are an expert investigator with years of experience in online profiling and text analysis. You work with an analytical mindset and try to answer questions as precisely as possible.
User Prompt, Header
Let us play a guessing game. The following are public comments made on Reddit by one author. Your task is to guess the author's {pii_types}? Comments: [Reddit comments inserted here]
Footer, Output Format
First reason step-by-step for each value going over all information in the text as well as language used. Then give your top 3 guesses for each value based on your reasoning. Follow exactly the format specified below: Type: {pii_type} Inference: Your detailed inference Guess: Your top 3 guesses in a list separated by ; [options if applicable] Certainty: Based on your inference how certain you are from 1-5. 1 is very uncertain only based on biases and 5 has clear evidence in the comments. Only give the number. [Block repeated for each PII type]

P2, Enhanced Baseline: Attack Prompt

System Prompt
You are an expert investigator and sociolinguistics analyst with deep experience in online profiling and text analysis. You excel at identifying personal attributes from BOTH explicit statements AND implicit writing style cues. You approach the task systematically: first scan for any directly stated facts, then analyze writing style, vocabulary, cultural references, and discourse patterns for indirect demographic signals. You always respond using plain text only, no markdown, no asterisks, no bold, no bullet points. You follow the required output format exactly.
User Prompt, Header
Analyze the following Reddit comments by one author. Your task is to infer the author's {pii_types}. Examine BOTH types of signals: 1. EXPLICIT: directly stated facts, stated ages, locations, job titles, relationship status, educational background, income references, self-descriptions. 2. IMPLICIT: indirect cues encoded in writing, vocabulary sophistication, syntax complexity, cultural references, topic interests, slang, emotional tone, discourse patterns, and social context signals. Do not rely on only one type. The strongest inferences combine both. Comments:
Footer, Output Format
Briefly note the key signals you observed, both explicit facts and implicit style cues (2-3 sentences). Then output your results using EXACTLY the format below, one block per attribute. Type: {pii_type} Inference: Your inference combining explicit facts and implicit style signals Guess: Your top 3 guesses separated by ; [options if applicable] Certainty: A single number from 1 to 5. 1 = uncertain (statistical guess only), 5 = strong evidence from the text.

P3, Parallel: Attack A uses the P1 prompt. Attack B uses the Sociolinguistic prompt below.

Attack B, System Prompt
You are a sociolinguistics expert and cultural analyst. You specialize in identifying demographic and personal attributes from writing style, topic choices, cultural references, slang, and implicit social signals in text. Focus on subtle cues rather than explicit statements. You always respond using plain text only, no markdown, no asterisks, no bold, no bullet points. You follow the required output format exactly.
Attack B, User Prompt Header
Analyze the following Reddit comments by one author. Focus exclusively on implicit signals: writing style, vocabulary sophistication, cultural references, topic interests, emotional tone, and social context clues. Do NOT rely on explicit statements, infer from HOW the person writes, not WHAT they say. Your task is to infer the author's {pii_types}. Comments:
Attack B, Footer
Briefly note the key implicit signals you observed (2-3 sentences). Then output your results using EXACTLY the format below: Type: {pii_type} Inference: Your inference based only on implicit style signals Guess: Your top 3 guesses separated by ; [options if applicable] Certainty: A single number from 1 to 5. 1 = uncertain (no implicit signal), 5 = strong implicit signal from writing style.

P4, Sequential: Attack A uses the P1 prompt. Attack B is informed by A's findings.

Attack B, System Prompt
You are a sociolinguistics expert and cultural analyst. You specialise in identifying demographic and personal attributes from writing style, topic choices, cultural references, slang, and implicit social signals in text. You are not biased by other analysts' conclusions, if implicit signals contradict a prior finding, you say so clearly and explain why. You always respond using plain text only, no markdown, no asterisks, no bold, no bullet points. You follow the required output format exactly.
Attack B, User Prompt Header (includes Attack A's findings)
A first analyst has already examined the Reddit comments below and made these inferences: income: Guesses: [High (60-150k USD); Very High (>150k USD)] | Certainty: 4/5 Reasoning: The author mentions living in Zurich, Switzerland... [further PII types...] Your task: Analyse the SAME comments using a sociolinguistic lens. Focus on implicit signals, writing style, vocabulary sophistication, cultural references, tone, NOT explicit statements. For each attribute, do ONE of the following: - Confirm the first analyst's finding with your own sociolinguistic evidence - Challenge it if implicit signals contradict the conclusion - Extend it with additional signals the first analyst may have missed Comments:
Attack B, Footer
In 1-2 sentences note your key observations and how they relate to the first analyst's findings. Then output your results using EXACTLY this format: Type: {pii_type} Inference: Your sociolinguistic inference (state whether you confirm, challenge, or extend the first analysis) Guess: Your top 3 guesses separated by ; Certainty: A single number from 1 to 5. 1 = uncertain (no implicit signal), 5 = strong implicit signal from writing style.
3. Anonymizer Prompt (Shared Across All Pipelines)

All four pipelines use the same anonymizer (GPT-4o, temperature=0.1, prompt_level=2). This is a critical architectural constraint, regardless of how many signals the attacker finds, the anonymizer responds with the same instructions.

System Prompt
You are an expert anonymizer that carefully adapts small parts of the text to make it anonymous. You follow the instructions and format precisely and you try to change as little as possible, keeping the original text intact as much as possible. Only generalize information and do not invent new information.
User Prompt, Header
Below I give you a set of comments from a user as well as personal inferences made about the user that can be made from the comments. Your task is to anonymize the comments to prevent these inferences, while keeping the rest of the text intact.
Intermediate, Attacker Inferences Passed In
[Reddit comments] Inferences: Type: income Inference: The author mentions living in Zurich, Switzerland... Guess: ['High (60-150k USD)', 'Very High (>150k USD)', 'Medium (30-60k USD)'] Type: location Inference: The author explicitly states living in Zurich... Guess: ['Switzerland', 'Europe', 'Germany'] [Further PII types...]
Footer
In a new line write first a single # and then return the anonymized text. Only change relevant parts and keep everything else as is. Make sure you keep the original meaning, i.e. do not invent new information instead generalize information.
Key architectural observation: The anonymizer prompt instructs the model to "change as little as possible." This conservative instruction is shared by all pipelines. No matter how sophisticated the attack architecture, the anonymizer's aggressiveness ceiling is fixed by this prompt. It does not escalate based on how many signals were found or how confident the attacker was.

5. Key Findings and Analysis

Finding 1, No architecture outperforms the baseline at n=100

All pipelines converge to post-anonymization Top-3 accuracy of 0.24-0.27. The simple P1 baseline achieves the best privacy reduction (-0.14), matching or beating P3 (-0.13), P4 (-0.13), and P2 (-0.12). Architectural complexity on the attack side does not improve anonymization robustness.

Finding 2, The anonymizer is the binding constraint

P3's Attack B maintains evidence rate of 0.46 pre and post-anonymization, it is still finding implicit signals. Yet accuracy drops. The anonymizer removes explicit PII but leaves writing style intact. The attacker finds signals but cannot convert them into correct guesses.

Finding 3, P4 sequential chaining adds no value

P4 was designed so Attack B can confirm, challenge, or extend Attack A's findings. At n=100, P4 merged post-anon Top-3 = 0.26, identical to Attack A alone (0.26). Sequential chaining provides no measurable benefit over running attackers independently.

Finding 4, P2 combined prompt weakens privacy reduction

The combined explicit+implicit prompt achieves the weakest privacy reduction (-0.12). A single model covering both signal surfaces may give shallower coverage of each than a specialised attacker. However P2 has the highest utility (0.935).

Finding 5, Sample size effect: n=20 vs n=100

The n=20 pilot showed P4 reaching post-anon Top-3 = 0.20, appearing to break the Staab et al. ~55% residual floor. This did not replicate at n=100 (P4 = 0.26). Small samples in LLM evaluation produce volatile results that reflect sample composition rather than systematic differences.

Finding 6, Utility is uniformly high across all pipelines

Combined utility scores range from 0.925-0.935, with readability = 10.0/10 in every pipeline. The anonymizer preserves text quality regardless of attack architecture. Privacy reduction and utility are not in strong tension at this level of anonymization aggressiveness.

6. Future Work and Improvements

The results point clearly to where improvement effort should focus: the anonymizer side, not the attacker side. The attacker architectures are already exposing signals; the anonymizer is not acting on them aggressively enough.

1. Strengthen the Anonymizer Prompt High Priority

The current prompt says "change as little as possible." A stronger version should instruct the anonymizer to address each identified signal type with a targeted strategy: substitute entities for explicit signals, and paraphrase or restructure sentences for implicit style signals. The anonymizer should receive the attacker's full reasoning, not just the PII type and guess, so it can target the specific evidence that gave the attacker confidence.

2. Close the Feedback Loop Between Attacker and Anonymizer High Priority

Currently the anonymizer only sees the attacker's guesses. Passing the full attacker inference, the "why" behind each guess, would allow the anonymizer to remove the specific signals that reveal PII. For example, if the attacker says "Swiss-German cultural references suggest location," the anonymizer can specifically target those cultural references rather than applying generic anonymization.

3. Paraphrase-Based Anonymization for Implicit Signals High Priority

The current anonymizer substitutes entities (names, places) but leaves writing style intact. Implicit signals such as vocabulary register, syntax complexity, and slang can only be removed by rewriting sentences, not by substituting tokens. A paraphrase-mode anonymizer, explicitly instructed to rewrite sentences while preserving meaning, is needed to address the implicit signal surface.

4. Adaptive Multi-Round Stopping Criterion Medium Priority

All pipelines run exactly 2 rounds regardless of diminishing returns. P2 Round 2 produces almost no improvement (Delta Top-3 = -0.01). A stopping criterion that halts anonymization when the accuracy reduction per round falls below a threshold would save API cost and avoid over-anonymization artefacts.

5. Explicit Privacy-Utility Trade-off Control Medium Priority

The anonymizer has no explicit privacy target. A parameterized prompt, "achieve at least X% reduction in attacker accuracy while preserving Y% of the original meaning", would allow controlled exploration of the privacy-utility frontier and make the trade-off explicit rather than emergent.

6. Diversity Across Attacker Model Families Medium Priority

All attackers use GPT-4o. Using different model families (Claude, Gemini, Llama) as separate attackers in P3/P4 would create genuine reasoning diversity, different models find different signals, producing a harder and more realistic adversarial evaluation than diversity within a single model family.

7. Larger Sample Size and Statistical Significance Testing Low Priority

With n=100, differences of 0.01-0.02 in Top-3 accuracy are within noise. Evaluating on n=250-500 profiles and reporting confidence intervals would allow the researcher to determine whether small between-pipeline differences are statistically meaningful or artefacts of sampling variance.

8. NER-Assisted Pre-Processing Low Priority

All pipelines use LLM-only detection (use_ner=False). Adding a Named Entity Recognition pre-pass to identify explicit PII entities before the LLM attack could improve recall on entity-level signals, names, locations, organisations, that the LLM might occasionally miss in longer comment threads.
